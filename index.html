About Code Blooded

Security-first. Inclusive by design.

We are a team of three from KamiLimu — a cybersecurity analyst, a full-stack engineer, and a forensic IT specialist. We build at the intersection of security and software, solving problems that affect real people.

🔐

Security-first by default

Every product is designed by people who study how systems break — threat intelligence, pen-testing, and fraud detection baked in from day one.

🌍

Inclusive by design

We champion women in Web3 and STEM through mentorship with AGCCI (UN Women), Siemens EmpowerHer, and GirlCode Kenya.

⚙️

Builders, not talkers

We ship, test, and iterate in public. Our work lives on GitHub — transparent, reproducible, and open to collaboration.

What We Do

Services built for a safer digital world

🔍 Security Assessments

Penetration testing, vulnerability analysis, and threat modelling for web and mobile applications.

💻 Web & Mobile Development

Full-stack products with React, React Native, Node.js, Express, MongoDB, Supabase, and Appwrite.

⛓️ Blockchain & Web3 Security

Scam-awareness workshops and security training for teams, students, and community organisations.
